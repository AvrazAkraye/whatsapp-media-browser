Clean-up ideas

Where the space is going. Nothing is deleted from here.

Everything here is read on this Mac; nothing is uploaded, and the page never connects to anything. Clicking a picture or video opens a preview; if your browser is not allowed to read a file, use Copy path and open it in Finder (⌘⇧G). To free space, delete media inside WhatsApp (Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage), not from the folder.
